## Planning

For non-trivial work, plan first. Every plan names its finish line, usually "ticket NN is
ready-for-human with `./mvnw verify` green".

## Long-run checklist

Keep the checklist in the ticket file under `docs/specs/redlink/tickets/` so it survives context
summarization. Tick items as they are done and add new ones as you find them.
