# 29 — 3D wow I: sol cycle, sky, tracks, rover animations, post-processing

Status: ready-for-human

## Scope

Make the scene feel alive while staying truthful: the rover still moves only on reports.

- **Sol cycle** (a Mars day, compressed to 12 minutes, from the wall clock so every viewer sees the
  same time of day):
  - the sun moves across the sky, low and blue-white at dusk (Mars sunsets are blue);
  - the sky goes from butterscotch through blue dusk to near-black night;
  - stars come out, Phobos (fast) and Deimos (slow) cross the sky, and Earth is a small blue light
    where the uplink beams come from;
  - the rover's headlights switch on at night;
  - a "Sol 14:20" clock is shown in the HUD;
  - dust storms darken all of it further.
- **Wheel tracks:** two dark ruts in the sand along the rover's real reported positions (the
  history from the telemetry API, plus every new report).
- **Rover animations**, driven by the command currently EXECUTING:
  - wheels turn while driving;
  - for TAKE_PHOTO, the mast head pans and a flash fires when the photo arrives;
  - for DRILL_SAMPLE, a drill arm lowers and dust puffs from the ground.
- **Post-processing:** bloom (beams, flash, Earth, lights glow) and a vignette.
  `@react-three/postprocessing@3.2.0` and `postprocessing@6.39.5`, exact versions at least two
  weeks old.
- Pure, tested maths for the sol (`scene/sol.ts`).

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Day, dusk and night look different; the clock matches the sun.
- [x] Tracks follow the reported path.
- [x] Wheels, mast, flash and drill animate for the matching commands.
- [x] Bloom and vignette; no console errors; build and tests green.
- [x] Checked visually at several times of day.
