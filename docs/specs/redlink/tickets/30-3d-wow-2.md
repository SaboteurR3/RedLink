# 30 — 3D wow II: intro flight, relay orbiter, dust storm, minimap, photo cones, alerts

Status: ready-for-human

## Scope

- **Intro flight** after login, once per browser tab session:
  - a starfield with Earth;
  - the camera leaves Earth and flies to Mars;
  - Mars grows until it fills the view;
  - a fade into the crater scene.

  About 7 seconds, with **Skip** (or Esc). Skipped automatically when the system asks for reduced
  motion, and only shown with the 3D view.
  - `scene/Intro.tsx` is lazy (2.7 kB; it shares the three.js chunk).
  - The decision is in `scene/introGate.ts`, so making it does not download three.js.
- **Relay orbiter:** a small satellite crossing the sky.
  - With relay windows on, it crosses exactly during each open window, so you can *see* why the
    uplink waits.
  - With windows off, it makes a decorative pass every 4 minutes.
- **Dust storm:** two rings of streaky dust (a canvas texture, no download) fade in around the crater
  and drift with the wind. This adds to ticket 29's darker sky and fog and the faster dust.
- **Minimap** (bottom right of the 3D view) shows the site in the 2D colours, with the rover, its
  heading, the target and the shown photo. Clicking it plans a drive there, like a click on the
  terrain.
- **Photo cone:** "Show on map" draws the camera's field of view from where the photo was taken, the
  way it looked. NAVCAM is 90° and MASTCAM is 40°, the same values as the rover's `PhotoRenderer`.
- **Safe-mode alert:** a pulsing red frame and a banner "SAFE MODE — only REBOOT is accepted" over
  the 3D view. The pulse stops when reduced motion is asked for.
- Pure, tested maths in `scene/orbit.ts`: orbiter progress and position, and the intro timeline.

## Notes for later tickets

- **Found and fixed:** the link status was loaded once, so with relay windows on the link panel
  counted down to "closes in 0 s" and stayed there. MissionControl now asks `GET /link` again just
  after each `nextChangeAt`. The orbiter needs this too.
- The API says when an open window *closes* but not when it *opened*. The orbiter uses the opening
  time announced while the window was closed, or the first time the page saw it open. If the
  window duration were in `WindowStatus`, this guess would not be needed.
- New demo switch: `?storm=1` in the address shows a dust storm (next to ticket 29's `?sol=`).

## Verification

- Frontend: tests (4 new for `orbit.ts`), type check, build.
- Browser check (`ui30.mjs`, headless Edge against the running apps). With no console errors:
  - the intro shows after login with its caption, Skip closes it, and it does not come back after a
    reload in the same tab or in a session that has seen it;
  - the minimap shows, and clicking it opens "Drive to (35, 28)" with a planned route;
  - "Show on map" draws a NAVCAM wedge facing the photo's heading (screenshot);
